Corporate Policies

Code of Conduct

Employees must maintain a respectful and professional workplace environment.

Data Security Policy

Confidential company information must not be shared outside the organization.

Employees must:

- Use company-approved devices
- Enable MFA
- Follow password security requirements

Remote Work Policy

Remote work is permitted with manager approval.

Employees are expected to:

- Be available during business hours
- Maintain a secure workspace
- Attend required meetings

Travel Policy

Business travel requires manager approval.

Receipts must be submitted within 10 business days of travel completion.

AI Usage Policy

Employees may use approved AI tools for business purposes.

Confidential customer data must not be entered into public AI systems.

Information Retention Policy

Business records must be retained according to legal and regulatory requirements.
