Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive:

- 20 vacation days annually
- 10 sick days annually
- 2 personal days annually

Unused vacation days may be carried forward for a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

Employees must submit vacation requests at least 2 weeks before the planned leave date.

Parental Leave

Employees are eligible for up to 18 months of parental leave as per company policy.

Benefits

Employees are enrolled in:

- Health Insurance
- Dental Insurance
- Vision Coverage
- Life Insurance

Benefits become active after successful completion of probation.

Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year in June and December.

Expense Reimbursement

Employees may claim up to $1000 annually for professional development courses.
